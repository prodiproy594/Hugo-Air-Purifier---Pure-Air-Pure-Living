an industry perspective

What They Don't Tell You
About HEPA Air Purifiers

For decades, traditional marketing has pushed HEPA as the gold standard. But here is the reality most air purifier brands rarely disclose: HEPA filters only trap particulate matter from the air, but they do not eliminate any VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds) such as smoke, odors or toxic fumes such as carbon monoxide from a autos, tools, gas appliances, furnaces, space heaters and burning fires. Also, tightly woven paper-fiber HEPA filters are prone to trapping moisture, which can turn into a breeding ground for mildew, trapped bacteria, and secondary odors right inside your living space over time. Furthermore, dense HEPA barriers often force loud, strained fan motors that consume excessive power and disrupt quiet bedrooms.

Hugo approaches air purity differently. By engineering an advanced 3-in-1 filtration ecosystem combining specialized particulate capture with high-density activated carbon, Hugo eliminates odors, toxic VOCs, and ambient pollutants whisper-quietly—without trapped moisture buildup and zero harmful ozone generation.
